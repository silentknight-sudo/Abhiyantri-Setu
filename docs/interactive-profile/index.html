Who we are

The Engineer's Bridge

Abhiyantri Setu connects the people who need building work done with the people who do it well.

The name joins Abhiyantri (engineering) and Setu (bridge). Homeowners, businesses and builders post a requirement once, receive quotations from verified providers, compare them clearly and manage the work from start to finish. The same platform grows into materials, equipment rental, professional services and business infrastructure.

Founded by Avinish Tripathi and headquartered in Greater Noida, the company starts in one city so it can know every provider and set standards category by category before expanding across India.

One Platform. The Entire Construction Ecosystem.

Vision

To become India's most trusted and comprehensive digital ecosystem for construction, engineering and infrastructure services.

Mission

To connect customers, professionals, contractors, suppliers and businesses through technology, transparency and reliable service delivery.

Promise

Whatever you need to build, repair or maintain, we connect you to verified people who will do it right, at a price you can understand.

Why we exist

Building in India still runs on word of mouth

Construction is one of India's largest industries and one of its least organised. Here is what changes with Abhiyantri Setu.

The usual way

  • Find a mason through a neighbour and hope for the best
  • Three verbal quotes in three formats that cannot be compared
  • Scope, payments and promises agreed only by phone
  • Materials bought from whoever answers, at unclear prices
  • Poor work shows up months later, with no one to call
  • Skilled tradespeople stay invisible beyond their street

With Abhiyantri Setu

  • Verified providers with checked documents and past work
  • Quotations in one standard format, side by side
  • Written scope, stage photos and milestone sign-off
  • Local supplier quotes by brand, grade and delivery
  • Ratings, complaint handling and after-service support
  • Genuine leads and a digital reputation for providers

Trust framework

Five layers of trust

Verification before listing. ID, PAN, GST where applicable, references and past-work review.
Structured quotations. Standard templates so customers compare like with like.
Documented execution. Written scope, stage photos and milestone sign-off.
Accountable support. Complaints acknowledged in 24 hours, a plan within 72.
Performance scorecards. Ratings, punctuality and rework tracked for every provider.

Verification confirms identity and documents at onboarding. It does not guarantee the outcome of work; the service contract is between customer and provider.
