# 2. Executive summary

India builds and repairs more than almost any country in the world, yet the way a family or a business gets building work done has barely changed. People still find a mason through a neighbour, collect verbal quotes that cannot be compared, buy cement from whoever answers the phone, and chase contractors who move on to the next site. Skilled tradespeople, meanwhile, depend on word of mouth for work and have no way to show their record.

**Abhiyantri Setu fixes this with one platform.** A customer describes a requirement once — "waterproof my terrace", "build a G+2 house on 200 sq yd", "rent scaffolding for 30 days", "install an STP in my factory" — and receives quotations from verified providers. The customer compares them side by side, hires with confidence, tracks progress and rates the work. Providers get genuine leads, a digital reputation and faster payment.

The company starts with two launch verticals in Greater Noida — **Build & Renovate** and **Quick Home Services** — and expands into **materials, equipment rental, professional services and business infrastructure** on the same platform. Each vertical feeds the others: a renovation needs tiles, a builder needs scaffolding, a finished home needs annual maintenance. Over time, the data from every completed job (scope, rates, materials, provider performance) makes matching, pricing and quality better than any directory can offer.

The business earns through commissions on completed work, lead fees, provider subscriptions, supply and rental margins, consultation fees, annual maintenance contracts and enterprise sourcing fees.

**Vision:** to become India's most trusted and comprehensive digital ecosystem for construction, engineering and infrastructure services.

# 5. The problem

## For homeowners and property owners

1. **Finding reliable people is hard.** Most providers are unbranded and found through word of mouth.
2. **Quotes cannot be compared.** One quote is per sq ft, another lump sum, another "material extra"; specifications are missing.
3. **No accountability.** Scope, payments and promises are verbal. Delays and disputes have no resolution path.
4. **Material buying is confusing.** Brands, grades and prices vary by shop; quantity estimates are guesswork.
5. **Quality is invisible until it fails.** Poor waterproofing, wiring or plumbing shows up months later.
6. **No record.** There is no history of what was done, by whom, with which materials, or under what warranty.

## For businesses, builders and institutions

7. **Vendor discovery is slow** for specialised work (STP, fire fighting, HVAC, automation).
8. **Bid comparison is manual** and inconsistent across technical and commercial terms.
9. **Maintenance is fragmented** across many small vendors with no single view.

## For providers, suppliers and equipment owners

10. **Limited visibility** beyond personal networks.
11. **Irregular work** and idle days between jobs.
12. **Payment delays** and disputes.
13. **No portable reputation** to win better customers.
14. **Idle equipment** that earns nothing between projects.

## Why the problem persists

Existing solutions cover parts of it. Home-service apps solve fixed-price, short jobs. Interior companies serve the premium modular segment. Directories sell leads but take no responsibility after the phone number is shared. B2B procurement platforms serve large builders. **No one connects the full lifecycle — find, compare, procure, build and maintain — for the ordinary homeowner, the small contractor and the mid-size business in one trusted place.**

# 6. The solution: how Abhiyantri Setu works

## The core idea

Every job starts as a **Requirement**. Customers create requirements; verified providers respond with **Quotations**; the customer compares and hires; the work is tracked to completion and rated. Materials and equipment plug into the same project.

## Customer journey

1. Visit the website or app and choose a category.
2. Describe the requirement: location, size, timeline, budget range.
3. Upload photos, drawings or documents.
4. The Abhiyantri Setu team checks the requirement and identifies suitable verified providers nearby.
5. Providers inspect the site where needed and submit quotations in a standard format.
6. Compare quotes side by side: scope, materials, brands, timeline, price, ratings.
7. Select a provider and confirm scope and payment terms in writing.
8. Work begins; receive progress updates and stage photos.
9. Approve completion.
10. Rate the provider and get after-service support or warranty help when needed.

## Provider journey (contractors, tradespeople, professionals)

1. Register with mobile number.
2. Build a profile: categories, service area, experience, past work photos.
3. Complete KYC and document verification (ID, PAN, GST where applicable, licences or professional registration).
4. Get approved and listed as a verified provider.
5. Receive relevant requirements in the service area.
6. Submit quotations, talk to customers and confirm jobs.
7. Complete work with documentation.
8. Receive payment and see earnings, ratings and performance on the dashboard.

## Supplier journey (materials)

Register → list catalogue with brands, grades and units → set delivery area and minimum order → receive RFQs and bulk enquiries → quote with freight and lead time → confirm order → deliver with proof → close and invoice.

## Equipment owner journey

Register equipment with photos, capacity and year → set rental rates, deposit, minimum billing and operator terms → manage availability → accept bookings → handover checklist → return inspection → deposit release and payout.

## Enterprise journey

Business account with GSTIN and multiple users → submit a project or RFQ with technical specification → receive bids from pre-qualified vendors → compare technical and commercial terms → negotiate → issue PO or contract → track milestones, quality and invoices.

## What the Abhiyantri Setu team does

Verifies providers, qualifies requirements, routes them to the right providers, sets category standards and quote templates, monitors timelines and quality, handles complaints and disputes, and continuously improves the provider network.

# 10. Trust and quality framework

Trust is the product. Abhiyantri Setu builds it in five layers.

1. **Verification before listing.** Identity (with consent), PAN, GST where applicable, address, references, past-work review; professional registration for architects and engineers (for example Council of Architecture registration). Annual re-verification.
2. **Structured quotations.** Standard quote templates per category (scope, materials, brands, quantities, timeline, payment terms) so customers can compare like with like.
3. **Documented execution.** Written scope before work starts, stage photos, milestone sign-offs and completion confirmation.
4. **Accountable support.** Complaints acknowledged within 24 hours with a resolution plan within 72 hours; rework first, refund where justified; escalation path for larger disputes.
5. **Performance management.** Provider scorecards on rating, response time, punctuality, rework and complaints. Coaching, then suspension, then removal.

**Data and privacy.** Personal data is collected with consent and protected in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Identity documents are stored securely with restricted access.

**Important disclaimer.** Verification confirms the identity and documents a provider supplies at onboarding. It does not guarantee the quality or outcome of any work. The service contract is between the customer and the provider. Abhiyantri Setu facilitates discovery, documentation, communication and dispute support.

*Proposed future programme:* "Setu Assured" — selected categories with workmanship warranty, milestone-based payments held by the platform and a re-work guarantee. [Not yet launched.]

## GMV versus revenue

Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) is the total value of work, materials and rentals transacted through the platform. Most of it belongs to providers. Abhiyantri Setu's **revenue** is only its commissions and fees. Investor material should always show both separately.

## Unit economics logic

Contribution per job = platform revenue − payment fees − support and operations cost − refunds and goodwill. Marketing and acquisition costs are then recovered through repeat jobs, cross-sell (renovation → materials → AMC) and subscriptions. The model becomes attractive when a customer uses more than one vertical and when the same provider completes many jobs.
